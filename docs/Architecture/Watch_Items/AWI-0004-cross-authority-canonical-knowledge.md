# AWI-0004 — Cross-Authority Canonical Knowledge

[Home](../../../README.md) › [Project Index](../../../PROJECT_INDEX.md) › [Architecture](../../README.md) › [Watch Items](README.md) › AWI-0004

> **Status:** Open
> **Date:** 2026-08-12
> **Owner:** Architecture Team

## Summary

Open architectural questions concerning how CRA-conformant knowledge systems maintained by different canonical authorities reference, consume, qualify, and interoperate with one another's canonical knowledge.

## Scope

This Watch Item covers concerns explicitly deferred in [AI Canonical Knowledge Interaction and Evolution](../../Discovery_Records/AI_Canonical_Knowledge_Interaction_and_Evolution.md) §27:

- **Knowledge authority boundaries**
- **Cross-repository identity**
- **Trust and provenance** for external canonical references
- **Versioning** across authorities
- **Conflicting authorities** and domain-specific qualification
- **External canonical references** between systems (e.g., EGLS consuming ELS electronics knowledge)

## Why Watch, Not Specification Now

- Discovery explicitly marks cross-domain authority questions as outside immediate scope
- Depends on identity model maturity (CRA-0002) and future relationship/trust architecture
- No adoption exercise has yet required cross-authority federation

## Candidate Workstream

May inform multiple future workstreams. Not assigned to a single candidate workstream at this time.
