## Summary

Open architectural questions concerning how canonical knowledge evolves through governed processes — including knowledge gaps, candidate knowledge, canonicalization policy, and re-evaluation — without uncontrolled mutation.

## Scope

This Watch Item consolidates the following related concerns from [AI Canonical Knowledge Interaction and Evolution](../../Discovery_Records/AI_Canonical_Knowledge_Interaction_and_Evolution.md):

- **Knowledge gaps** — whether gaps are first-class entities, managed artifacts, implementation metadata, or derived diagnostics
- **Candidate knowledge** — architectural distinction from accepted canonical knowledge until canonicalization
- **Canonicalization policy** — architectural concept for rules governing acceptance (not a detailed policy schema)
- **Controlled evolution lifecycle** — gap/conflict/evidence → candidate → evaluation → evolved canonical knowledge
- **Re-evaluation and supersession triggers** — how new evidence challenges existing canonical knowledge; interaction with CRA-0002 lineage

## Why Watch, Not Specification Now

- Representation of knowledge gaps is undecided
- Canonicalization policy schema is premature
- Relationship between evolution mechanics and existing CRA-0002 supersession needs analysis
- May depend on applicability/epistemic modeling (AWI-0002) and relationship architecture
