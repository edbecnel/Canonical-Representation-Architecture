# AWI-0003 — AI-Assisted Knowledge Evaluation

> **Status:** Open

> **Owner:** Architecture Team

## Summary

Open architectural questions concerning AI-assisted and automated participation in knowledge evaluation and canonicalization — including independent evaluators, evidence aggregation, and the separation of reasoning from canonical authority.

## Scope

This Watch Item consolidates the following related concerns from [AI Canonical Knowledge Interaction and Evolution](../../Discovery_Records/AI_Canonical_Knowledge_Interaction_and_Evolution.md):

- **AI-assisted canonicalization** — scale without human approval of every candidate
- **Independent evaluation** — multiple models, agents, humans, and tools as evidence sources
- **Evidence aggregation** — evaluations as evidence, not simple majority votes
- **Evaluator independence** — what constitutes sufficient independence between evaluators
- **Reasoning vs authority** — no reasoning agent inherently possesses canonical authority
- **AI knowledge normalization** — transforming poorly scoped candidates into appropriately qualified canonical knowledge

## Why Watch, Not Specification Now

- Depends on canonicalization architecture (AWI-0001)
- Technology-independent role taxonomy not finalized
- Aggregation model for conflicting evaluations unresolved
- CRA must remain independent of current commercial AI systems

## Foundational Questions for CRA-0001 Evaluation

Is canonical authority independent of any particular reasoning agent already entailed by FP-4 (Governance Designation), or does it require explicit architectural treatment?

**CRA-0001 must not be modified normatively until evaluated.**
